# Ripple Citation Canon

**Location in repo:** `docs/citation-canon.md`

## How this file works

A citation's verification status is **durable**. Once an entry is in this file, it has been checked against PubMed or the publisher by a human or by a session with network access, and it is cleared for use on any Ripple page.

**Rules for any agent or contributor:**

1. A citation **in this file** may be used as-is. Do not re-verify it. Do not remove it because the current environment can't reach the publisher.
2. A citation **not in this file** must be verified against PubMed or the publisher before it goes on a page. If verification isn't possible in the current environment, **flag it and stop** — do not add it, and do not silently substitute a claim it was meant to support.
3. Network failure is **never** grounds for removing an existing entry. A blocked proxy is not evidence a paper doesn't exist.
4. Adding an entry requires the PMID or DOI and the date it was verified.

## Verified entries

### Menopause staging and cycle change

**Harlow SD, Gass M, Hall JE, et al.** Executive summary of the Stages of Reproductive Aging Workshop +10: addressing the unfinished agenda of staging reproductive aging. *J Clin Endocrinol Metab.* 2012;97(4):1159–68. doi:10.1210/jc.2011-3362. PMID 22344196.
Also published simultaneously in *Menopause.* 2012;19(4):387–95.
*Verified 2026-08-06.* Source of the STRAW+10 staging criteria: early transition = persistent ≥7-day difference in length of consecutive cycles; late transition = amenorrhea ≥60 days.

**Harlow SD.** Menstrual cycle changes as women approach the final menses: what matters? *Obstet Gynecol Clin North Am.* 2018;45(4):599–611. doi:10.1016/j.ogc.2018.07.003. PMID 30401545.
*Verified 2026-08-06.* **Single author.** Sole source for the 12–25% figure (proportion of women with minimal or no change in cycle length before the final menstrual period). Do not attribute that statistic to any other entry.

**Harlow SD, Paramsothy P.** Menstruation and the menopausal transition. *Obstet Gynecol Clin North Am.* 2011;38(3):595–607. doi:10.1016/j.ogc.2011.05.010. PMID 21961722. PMCID PMC3232023.
*Verified 2026-08-06.* Optional background only. **Superseded by Harlow 2018 for anything a Ripple page claims** — prefer 2018 and consider omitting this entry entirely.

> ⚠️ **Transposition hazard.** Harlow 2011 (38(3):595–607) and Harlow 2018 (45(4):599–611) share an author, a journal, a topic, and near-identical page ranges. Check the year and PMID whenever either appears.

### Symptom instruments

**Greene JG.** Constructing a standard climacteric scale. *Maturitas.* 1998;29(1):25–31.
*Verified in earlier session; carried forward.* Source for the 21-item Greene Climacteric Scale and its scoring method. **Licensing status unresolved** — see the open RWS and Mapi threads.

**Goldstein S.** An efficient tool for the primary care management of menopause. *Can Fam Physician.* 2017;63(4):295–8.
*Verified 2026-08-06.* Source publication for the MQ6. Cite alongside any outbound MQ6 link. **Do not reproduce MQ6 content** — link only.
